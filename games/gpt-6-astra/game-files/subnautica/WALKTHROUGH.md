# Adaptation walkthrough — story spoilers

This guide describes this browser build's condensed route, including its departures. It is not a guide asserting exact original Subnautica 1.0 mechanics or coordinates.

1. **Establish oxygen and tools.** Collect titanium, copper, quartz and acid mushrooms around Lifepod 5. Surface between dives. Craft a standard tank from three titanium and equip it in the PDA. Craft a battery from copper and two acid mushrooms, then a scanner from that battery and titanium. Catch Peepers for cooking and Bladderfish for water.
2. **Restore the radio.** Kelp contains creepvine seed clusters for silicone rubber. Bring rubber, cave sulfur and titanium to the fabricator for a repair tool. Interact with the lifepod radio while carrying it. Craft a knife as well: harvested plant samples require it.
3. **Follow distress signals and scan fragments.** Lifepod 3 and nearby debris provide the Seaglide and mobile vehicle bay route. Scan the actual fragments by aiming and holding the left mouse button. Explore the red-grass wreck area near Lifepod 17 for Seamoth and laser cutter fragments. The exact sites are fixed, not randomized. The PDA's blueprint panel shows discovery counts.
4. **Build transport and a refuge.** Fabricate a vehicle bay, deploy it at the surface through Inventory, then interact to construct a Seamoth. Build a habitat builder, room, hatch, solar panels, lockers and fabrication/upgrading equipment. A hatch is required to enter. Carry spare batteries and cells. Store intermediate materials while making the larger recipes; inventory area matters.
5. **Investigate Mountain Island.** Follow the signal north. Collect a purple tablet around the alien structure and use its terminal. Infection blocks shutdown. The structure identifies further exploration routes. The adaptation grants progression through the terminal rather than a faithful security-door sequence.
6. **Visit the Aurora.** Carry radiation protection, a laser cutter and a repair tool. The interaction entrance is toward the near/front side of the large exterior model. The captain's access code is **2679**. Stabilize the drive core and retrieve Neptune plans. The browser interior is a short substitute for the ship's original deck routes. Prawn blueprints become available here.
7. **Trace the Degasi sites.** Visit the Floating Island habitat, then the 250 m Jellyshroom habitat and 500 m Deep Grand Reef habitat. Logs lead onward; modification, stasis and reinforced-suit blueprints are available through exploration. Not every optional visit is a hard prerequisite.
8. **Prepare for depth.** Seamoth depth upgrades top out at 900 m here. Use a Prawn or Cyclops with appropriate modules for the lava areas. Install modules from Inventory while near the matching vehicle. Make a deep refuge, take cells and healing supplies, and carry purple tablets. Avoid direct leviathan approaches; retreat is viable after an attack. Cyclops silent running lowers attention at additional energy cost.
9. **Enter the Lost River.** The adaptation has marked deep entrances through Blood Kelp Trench, northern Blood Kelp, Deep Grand Reef and the Bulb side. Follow a continuous descending cavern, not straight down through the surface terrain. The disease research terminal consumes a purple tablet and reveals the thermal route. Collect nickel and deeper mineral resources.
10. **Reach the Alien Thermal Plant.** Continue down the marked thermal passage. The plant terminal consumes a purple tablet, gives a blue tablet, and unlocks ion power. Leave an inventory cell available for the tablet. Kyanite is available in the lava passage. The temperatures and damage rules are simplified.
11. **Activate containment.** Continue to the Lava Lakes and containment facility. Bring a blue tablet and an ion cube. Activate the incubator to unlock hatching enzymes. Its five ingredients are eye stalk seed (Grand Reef), ghost weed seed (Lost River), fungal sample (Mushroom Forest), bulb bush sample (Bulb Zone) and sea crown seed (near containment). Use a knife to collect applicable samples. The recipe is made at a fabricator.
12. **Hatch and cure.** Return with the enzymes, use the incubator and then the enzyme interaction. The portal interaction gives a shortened return to the surface. Return to the quarantine terminal and disable it after the cure.
13. **Build and launch Neptune.** The mobile vehicle bay makes the platform after its plans are unlocked. Interact with that platform to construct gantry, boosters, fuel reserve and cockpit in order. The recipes require the advanced material chain and ion cells. Launch becomes available after quarantine shutdown. The build saves immediately before launch, shows an ascent and adapted ending/credits, and permits return to the pre-launch save.

This chain is present and its resource/station prerequisites pass a rules-level completion test. The entire route has not been demonstrated in an uninterrupted fresh-save 3D playthrough. If a route fails, use Save/Export to preserve the state and record the nearest signal/biome and what happened. Creative is an inspection mode, not evidence of Survival completion.
