# Reference ledger

Selected release: **Subnautica, original PC/Steam 1.0, 23 January 2018**. No specific Steam depot/hash was obtained. An archived executable, original data files and exhaustive release-matched footage were not available in this work. Therefore the release is documented, but comprehensive 1.0 fidelity is not verified.

Sources accessed 4 September 2026. The distinction between observation and implementation matters: a source may confirm that a feature belongs to Subnautica without proving that this adaptation reproduces it accurately.

## Limited or unsuccessful evidence

Search-index excerpts for [Mapping Subnautica](https://subnautica.fandom.com/wiki/Mapping_Subnautica), [Grassy Plateaus](https://subnautica.fandom.com/wiki/Grassy_Plateaus) and [Lost River](https://subnautica.fandom.com/wiki/Lost_River) informed further checking. Direct Fandom page access was blocked. The excerpts mentioned plateau depths around 40–200 m, multiple red-grass regions and seven Lost River sub-biomes. These are secondary, partial readings; they do not verify the adaptation's terrain or all original names/subdivisions.

An official cinematic trailer and a GDC postmortem were located, but were not used as fully inspected evidence for implementation details. A hatching-enzyme item URL could not be retrieved. The exact cure recipe, access-tablet requirements, original event order, story text and ending were not fully independently verified from those sources.

## Application of evidence

The reference observations informed the broad east/west placement of major regions, recognizable creature/tool cues, red versus green vegetation, mushroom cap color, shallow/deep palette differences and several central recipes. Terrain, fog numbers, creature sizes, interior layouts, animation and sound are authored approximations. The supporting community pages may reflect later original-game updates.

The adaptation deliberately adds an objective panel, biome label, cave-mouth waypoints and simplified story guidance. These are usability departures, not features asserted to exist in the original interface. The world uses X east and Z south internally; the original map coordinates should not be treated as interchangeable with the adaptation's coordinates.

`COVERAGE.md` is the feature-by-feature record. No visual match score, comprehensive reference-viewpoint comparison, licensed original asset inventory or complete release verification is claimed.
