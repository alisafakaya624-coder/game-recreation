Cataclysm - a natural disaster survival demo
Built entirely by Claude Opus 5.5 for the AIPilled YouTube channel.

HOW TO PLAY
1. Unzip this folder.
2. Double-click natural-disaster-survival.html to open it in Chrome or Edge (recommended).
3. You need an internet connection the first time it loads (the 3D engine, three.js, is fetched from a CDN).

Controls are shown in-game. A desktop/laptop with a decent GPU gives the best frame rate.
